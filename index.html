Activiews

Better fitness visuals for your Apple workouts

Activiews is a privacy-first fitness companion for your Apple workouts that helps you visualize and track your activities.

Key Features

Turn every workout into a nice visual story while keeping your data private and secure

Beautiful Workout Maps & Cards

Create high-resolution maps and themed cards for each activity: perfect for sharing or keeping as motivation.

Workout Flyover

View your workouts from a dynamic top-down perspective, replaying your path with engaging animations.

Activities Calendar

See your workouts as a heatmap across months, tracking your progress over time.

Private by Design

Reads your data securely from the Apple Health app. No account registration needed. No server communication. Nothing leaves your device.

Dark & Light Themes

Customize your exports and in-app visuals to suit your mood or style, with adaptive themes and color pickers.

Easy Export & Sharing

Save maps and cards to your photo library or share them with friends.

Stats That Matter

See distance, duration, calories burned, and heart rate stats with clean presentations.

Flexible Pricing

Choose what works for you: lifetime access for €29.99 or monthly pro subscription for €1.99. No hidden fees, cancel anytime.
